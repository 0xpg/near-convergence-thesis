About Bitwise NEAR ETF (NRR)

What the data says

NRR is the only US spot NEAR ETF on our list, so every dollar of NEAR ETF flow so far is its own. It began trading on 29 Sep 2026 and has taken in $79.1M since then. Bitwise reports net assets of $52.6M.

NRR is a staking fund: it stakes NEAR it holds to earn network rewards.

In the last 30 trading days to 12 Nov 2026 it took in $21.0M; the whole NEAR group took in $21.0M.

Holders of NRR are behind: their average entry of $4.996 sits above the 12 Nov 2026 close of $3.486, a paper loss of −30.2% (−$23.2M).

Inflow days outnumber outflow days 17 to 5 in our record since 29 Sep 2026, with 11 flat days. The record day brought in $36.0M on 29 Sep 2026; the heaviest redemptions, $4.8M, came on 27 Oct 2026.

NRR charges 0.75% a year. There is no other US spot NEAR ETF to compare it with yet; across all 58 crypto ETFs we track, the median fee is 0.25%.

NRR ETF — FAQ
