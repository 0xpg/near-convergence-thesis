04 why our data

Why our ETF flow data

One method for every fund, published every trading day.

Fund by fund, not just totals.

Every day for every fund: IBIT, FBTC, ETHA, BSOL, GDOG and 53 more.

Calculated from issuer data.

Daily flow = change in shares outstanding × NAV, from the figures each issuer publishes for its fund. One method for all 58 funds.

Published before the US open.

One issue per trading day, monitored at status.cryptoetf.today.

09 faq

Crypto ETF data partnerships — FAQ

What does a partnership cost?

It depends on what you need. Terms are agreed individually — for many uses a credit and a link is enough.

How fresh is the data?

Once per trading day, for the previous day, before the US open (UTC morning).

Can I get more than 30 days of history?

Yes, full history since January 2024 is available to partners.

Which assets and funds?

BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, HYPE, LINK, HBAR, AVAX, DOGE, LTC, SUI, DOT, BNB, NEAR, TRX, ZEC — every US-listed spot ETF on each.

Do you cover new ETFs as they launch?

Yes. Every new US spot crypto ETF is added to the tracker, usually within days of its first trade.

Can the widget be in my language?

The site runs in 18 languages; tell us yours.

Is this investment advice?

No. It is market data.
